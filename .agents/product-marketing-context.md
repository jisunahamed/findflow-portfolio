# FindFlow — Product Marketing Context

## Company profile

FindFlow is an AI-first software company and product engineering partner for
ambitious startups, SMEs, and digital teams serving the European market.

The company turns operational bottlenecks and product ideas into useful digital
systems. One integrated team covers discovery, product design, engineering,
launch, and ongoing improvement.

## Positioning

**Category:** AI automation and software development company

**Primary audience:** European startups, SMEs, SaaS teams, operations leaders,
and innovation teams that need senior product and engineering capacity.

**Core promise:** Move from a costly manual process or product idea to reliable,
production-ready software with one accountable team.

**Differentiators:**

- Product strategy, UX design, and engineering in one delivery team
- AI applied to a defined workflow or customer problem, not added for novelty
- Privacy, accessibility, security, and maintainability considered from discovery
- Clear weekly progress, documented decisions, and client ownership of the result
- Planned collaboration windows for UK and Central European teams

## Service architecture

1. **AI Automation**
   - AI agents and internal copilots
   - Workflow and document automation
   - CRM, ERP, and third-party integrations
   - Knowledge assistants and support automation
2. **Web Development**
   - High-performance marketing websites
   - Web applications and customer portals
   - E-commerce experiences
   - Technical SEO, accessibility, and analytics
3. **Product Design**
   - Product strategy and discovery
   - User journeys, UX, and interface design
   - Interactive prototypes and usability testing
   - Design systems
4. **Software Development**
   - Custom business software
   - Frontend, backend, and API engineering
   - Cloud architecture, DevOps, QA, and modernisation
5. **SaaS Development**
   - MVP definition and development
   - Authentication, billing, and multi-tenant architecture
   - Analytics, administration, and integrations
   - Launch, iteration, and scaling

## Brand voice

Clear, calm, technically credible, and commercially aware. Use plain English and
active voice. Prefer specific outcomes and delivery details to exaggerated claims.
Never invent client names, testimonials, project counts, revenue, compliance
certifications, or quantified results.

## Messaging hierarchy

**Primary message:** AI automation and software built around how your business
actually works.

**Supporting message:** FindFlow combines product thinking, design, and
engineering to build AI workflows, websites, custom software, and SaaS products
for European teams.

**Primary CTA:** Plan your project

**Secondary CTA:** Explore services

## SEO direction

**Primary topic:** AI automation and software development company for Europe

**Supporting topics:**

- AI automation services
- custom software development
- SaaS development company
- web development for European businesses
- product design and development
- AI workflow automation
- software development partner

Avoid keyword stuffing and unverified location claims such as “EU-based” or
“UK-based.” Use “for European teams” and “serving Europe” instead.

## Market rationale

- Eurostat reported that 20% of EU enterprises used AI in 2025, up from 13.5% in
  2024, showing fast-growing demand for practical implementation.
  https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/web/products-eurostat-news/w/ddn-20251211-2
- 52.7% of EU enterprises used paid cloud services in 2025, while application
  development, CRM, and computing platforms remain important adoption areas.
  https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/web/products-eurostat-news/w/ddn-20260203-1
- In 2023, 57.5% of EU enterprises recruiting ICT specialists reported
  difficulty filling those roles, supporting a partner-led delivery model.
  https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/statistics-explained/SEPDF/cache/40327.pdf
- GDPR encourages data protection by design and by default from the earliest
  stages of product development.
  https://commission.europa.eu/law/law-topic/data-protection/information-business-and-organisations/obligations/what-does-data-protection-design-and-default-mean_en
- The European Accessibility Act entered into application in June 2025 for
  covered products and services, making accessible product delivery a stronger
  buying consideration.
  https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/news/eu-becomes-more-accessible-all
- The AI Act’s staged application makes transparency, risk awareness, and
  responsible AI delivery increasingly important for European buyers.
  https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/regulatory-framework-ai
